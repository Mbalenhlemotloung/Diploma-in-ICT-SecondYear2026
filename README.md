# 🎓 Diploma in ICT Learning Journey

Hi, I'm **Mbalenhle Motloung**, a Diploma in ICT (Application Development) student at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology (CPUT).

I created this repository to document my learning journey throughout my diploma. Instead of only keeping my work on my computer, I wanted one place where I could track my progress, reflect on what I've learned and see how my skills develop over time.

Throughout my studies, I'll be uploading lecture notes, practical exercises, assignments, projects and personal reflections. I'll also document the challenges I face, the mistakes I make and how I solve them because I believe that learning is about the process not just the final result.

My goal is to stay consistent, improve a little every day and build projects that help me apply what I learn in class. By the time I graduate I hope this repository will show not only what I know but also how I've grown as a developer and problem solver.

During my diploma, I'll be studying modules including Applications Development Fundamentals2, Applications Development Practical2, Communications Network Fundamentals2, Data Science, Multimedia, Information Management2, Information Analysis2 and Project Management2. Each module will have its own section containing notes, practical work, exercises, and any projects I complete along the way.

As I continue learning, I'll be working with technologies such as Java, Python, SQL, Git, GitHub, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SQL , figma. I'll update this repository whenever I learn something new or build something worth sharing.

I'll also keep a learning journal where I reflect on what I studied each week, the concepts I found challenging, how I overcame them and the lessons I want to remember. My hope is that these reflections will help me become a better learner and allow me to look back at how far I've come.

Some of the projects you'll find here include my Student Management System, Matric Gateway project, GlowEnhle website, FreshSip website, portfolio website, and other projects that I develop during my studies.

Thank you for visiting my repository. Whether you're another student, a recruiter, or someone who simply enjoys technology, I hope my journey encourages you to keep learning and building. Every project in this repository represents another step towards becoming the developer I aspire to be.
